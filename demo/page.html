Terminal: the pictures as Rasters, as the demo mod draws them

    

    
The terminal draws no Svg element, so the mod's pane draws each picture as a Raster: its shapes on
      dots, two across and four down a cell, each cell a block, a quarter or a braille character in two colours, and its
      labels in letters. This page paints the cells as a terminal does, from the same cells the mod sends.
